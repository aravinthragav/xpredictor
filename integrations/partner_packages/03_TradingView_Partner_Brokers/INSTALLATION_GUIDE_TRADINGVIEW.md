# TradingView Partner Broker Integration Guide
### Institutional Pine Script v5 & Webhook Deployment

This package is designed for top-tier retail brokers with official TradingView integrations (e.g. **Pepperstone, IC Markets, BlackBull Markets, Vantage, Eightcap, OANDA, Axi**).

---

## 1. Package Files
- `XPredictor_RealityCone.pine` — TradingView Pine Script v5 Source Code

---

## 2. Installation into TradingView (2 Minutes)
1. Open any chart on [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/).
2. At the bottom panel, click on **Pine Editor**.
3. Clear existing text, and paste the entire contents of `XPredictor_RealityCone.pine`.
4. Click **Save** and then click **Add to Chart**.
5. The forward **Bayesian Reality Cone** and **Institutional Telemetry HUD** will render immediately on the latest live candle.

---

## 3. Commercial Distribution to Broker Clients
Brokers can distribute this script to their clients in three formats:
1. **Invite-Only Script on TradingView**: Publish as an "Invite-Only" community script, granting instant access to client TradingView usernames upon funding their live account.
2. **Client Portal Embed**: Embed our standalone web terminal in the broker's client portal (`?partner=pepperstone`).
3. **Webhook Signal Alerts**: Broadcast automated TradingView alerts directly to clients' Discord, Telegram, or SMS.
